# Winnow website imagery: generation prompts

Paste the whole block below into your generator. Each asset is self-contained (the style anchor is repeated inside every prompt), so you can also paste them one at a time. Save outputs with the exact file names given and put them all in one folder.

Formats: stills as JPEG at the stated size. Videos as MP4 (H.264), no audio, seamless or near-seamless loop, plus a JPEG poster frame with the same base name. If your tool caps at 1920 wide or 5 seconds, that is fine.

---

GLOBAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR THIS SET

Produce 11 assets for one website. They must look like they were shot on the same trip: the same desert, the same camera, the same colour grade. Photoreal desert photography in the style of the Namib: red-orange dunes with sharp light-and-shadow ridges, warm golden-hour light, deep blue-violet shadows, thin haze on the horizon, clear sky or a few thin clouds. Cinematic, calm, wide. Full-frame camera look, 35 to 50 mm, f/8, natural colour, no HDR look, no oversaturation, subtle film grain.

Never include: people, animals, vehicles, buildings unless the prompt asks for them, text, letters, logos, watermarks, lens flare, sun disc, fantasy elements, cartoon or painting styles, neon or purple skies.

Videos: locked-off or single slow constant-speed camera move as described. No cuts, no zooms unless stated, nothing enters or leaves frame, constant light so the clip loops.
