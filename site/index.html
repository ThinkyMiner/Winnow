Know Before You Click.

Winnow tells you whether an article or video is worth your time before you open it. Read, skim, save, or skip, with a confidence, tuned to what you actually care about.

Add to Chrome View on GitHub

Your attention is expensive. The internet acts like it isn't.

Decisions, Not Summaries

A summary still costs you the read. Winnow returns one of four decisions, READ, SKIM, SAVE or SKIP, with a confidence, so the choice is made before the tab is.

Typed Answers, Not Prose

Every card is built from eleven typed questions answered by a decision model. The reasons you see are templates we wrote, filled with numbers it returned. Nothing on the card is generated text.

For This Reader

The same page is not worth the same to everyone. Winnow judges against your goals, your recent topics and what you have read or skipped, so the verdict is yours.

It works where you already browse.

Hacker News, YouTube, Any Link-Heavy Page

In feed mode Winnow reads the titles and snippets already on the page, judges up to twelve links in a single call, and drops a pill next to each one. Reload and nothing is sent: verdicts are cached for seven days.

Hover For The Card. Nothing On The Page Moves.

Pills are rendered in a shadow DOM, sized to the line they sit on. Hover or focus one for the reasons. The page you are reading keeps its layout, its scroll position and its scripts.

Skip to the part that matters.

Payload Location

For a video with captions, Winnow asks where the useful part sits and which caption segment it starts in. The card shows the range and a link that seeks the player to it.

Honest Limit

No captions, no skip-to. Winnow still judges the title and description, but it will not guess a timestamp it cannot see.

Useful to you, not useful in general.

What Reader State Is

Three things: the goals you type into the options page, a rolling window of topics you have recently read, and the read/skip feedback you give on cards. All of it stays in the extension's local storage.

Change Your Goals, Change The Verdicts

Reader state is part of every question, so editing your goals invalidates the cache. The next page you open is judged fresh against the new you.

Decisions, not essays.

Choice

One option from a fixed set, with a probability for each. Content type, payload location, the verdict itself.

Noul

A yes/no as a probability between 0 and 1. Already known to you? Undisclosed sales pitch? AI-written?

Score

A position on an ordered scale we define, like insight density from 0 to 2, with a confidence.

Winnow is powered by Jev, a decision model from TypeSafe that returns typed answers, never free text. Every sentence on a card is a template we wrote.

What You Get

A Chrome MV3 extension, MIT licensed, at github.com/ThinkyMiner/Winnow. No backend. No analytics, no telemetry. Your key stays in local storage.

Where Your Text Goes

Page text goes to api.typesafe.ai and nowhere else. This is early access: expect rough edges, and read the privacy note before you install.

Install

  1. 01 Download

    Get the latest release zip and unzip it anywhere you will not delete.

  2. 02 Load Unpacked

    Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.

  3. 03 Paste Your Jev Key

    Create one at console.typesafe.ai. About a tenth of a cent per Hacker News page.

  4. Chrome Web Store: coming soon.

Questions

How much does it cost?

About $0.0015 for a 30-link Hacker News front page, measured against jev-1.13.0. A single article or video is a fraction of that. Verdicts are cached for seven days, so reloads cost nothing.

How accurate is it?

80% verdict agreement on our 40-fixture held-out split. The goldens are under review, so treat that as a floor we are still checking, not a benchmark.

What about paywalls?

Winnow judges what it can see. On a paywalled page that is the teaser, so the verdict is about the teaser and says so.

Videos without captions?

You still get a verdict from the title and description, but there is no skip-to. Winnow does not guess timestamps it cannot read.

Other Chromium browsers?

Anything that loads MV3 unpacked extensions should work: Edge, Brave, Arc. We test on Chrome.

Firefox?

No, not yet.

Does it slow pages down?

No. Pills render in a shadow DOM and every call is asynchronous. The page finishes loading exactly as it would without Winnow.

Can I change the questions?

Yes. They live in src/jev/questions.ts. Edit, rebuild, reload the extension.
